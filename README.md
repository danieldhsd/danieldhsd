### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer at Nubank**, designing and building scalable, resilient, and high-performance distributed systems that support critical financial products used by millions of customers.

- 🟣 **Software Engineer at Nubank**: Focusing on system design, distributed architectures, high consistency, fault tolerance, and backend platform evolution using **Clojure** and **Datomic**.
- 🟢 **Ex-PicPay (Nearly 4 years)**: Specialized in designing and scaling high-throughput backend services for core financial lending products (*"Empréstimo com Garantia do FGTS"* and vehicle-secured loans) using **Java**, **Spring Boot**, **Microservices**, and **Kubernetes**.
- 🌾 **Ex-Syngenta Digital (via Framework Digital)**: Developed distributed cloud services, serverless solutions (**AWS Lambda**), and microservices with **Java/Spring** and **Python/FastAPI**.
- 🛰️ **Ex-Sillion**: Built real-time IoT vehicle telemetry data ingestion pipelines for large fleet management systems.
- 🎓 **Education**: Bachelor's Degree in **Computer Science** from *Universidade de Itaúna* (2016 – 2019).
- 📍 **Location**: Belo Horizonte, MG — Brazil.
- 💬 **Passions**: Distributed Systems, System Design, Domain-Driven Design (DDD), Clean Architecture, Observability, and Operational Excellence.
